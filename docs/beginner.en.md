# Beginner

!!! tip "What Beginner is"
    **Beginner** is a standalone menu for newcomers that works without any settings or presets. Even if you have not registered any birth data, you can type it in on the spot and start reading right away. Simply reading down the page teaches you how to read a birth chart (natal chart) naturally.

!!! note "No settings needed"
    Unlike the other charts, Beginner does not require you to choose a preset. The planets and aspects it displays are fixed for beginners, so you can forget about settings and concentrate on reading.

## Birth Data and Chart

![The birth data entry form. Enter name, date of birth, time of birth, and birthplace, then press Show Chart to create the chart](assets/beginner-01-birth-data.jpg)

### Steps

1. Open **Beginner**, the first item in the main menu.
2. Prepare the birth data. Either **choose someone from the picker in the header** or **type it in directly** (there is no need to register it in advance — you can create a chart immediately).
3. Click **Show Chart** and the **natal chart appears in a large single wheel** just below. The **Change color** button at the top right switches the color scheme (standard pastel / old StarNavigator purple) with a single tap.
4. The **Open Natal Interpretation Report** button just below the chart opens a detailed natal interpretation report (Plus plan and above).
5. Scrolling further down brings you to **Reading Your Nature** and **Reading by Theme**.

### Saving the birth data you entered

- Press **Save As New** below the entry fields and the birth data you typed is stored as **registered birth data**. From then on it appears in the header picker and in the birth data list, and you can use it as-is in the Natal, Synastry, and other charts.
- If you loaded a saved person and then corrected the details, **Overwrite** updates that record (use **Save As New** when you want to register it as a different person).
- The **Save As New** and **Overwrite** buttons appear **when you are logged in (free plan and above)**.
- The **Show Chart** button only recalculates the chart; it does not save the birth data. Use the buttons above when you want to save it.

## Reading Your Nature

Below the chart are sections for reading the nature you were born with.

### Planets and signs

- **The Sun, the Moon, and the Ascendant (ASC)** are shown as large cards with their meanings.
- The other planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars, and so on) are listed together, and **clicking one shows its meaning**.
- If the birth time is unknown, the ASC cannot be determined, so the ASC card is not shown.
- Also, when the birth time is unknown the Moon is calculated as if the birth were at noon, so it can be off by up to 6-7 degrees and may fall in a different sign.

### Quality and element balance

- The balance of Fire, Earth, Air, and Water (the elements) and of Cardinal, Fixed, and Mutable (the qualities) is shown with commentary.

## Reading by Theme

When the birth time is known, hints for reading by theme are shown.

![Reading by Theme. Choosing Money from the six themes opens guidance text, and the rows and columns of the relevant planets are colored in the aspect table below](assets/beginner-02-theme.jpg)

### Steps

1. Choose one of the **themes** (Career, Money, Love and Marriage, Health, Home and Family, and Relationships and Communication — six in all).
2. Guidance text opens under the theme you chose, showing the houses, rulers, and planets that matter for that theme, taken from that specific chart.
3. At the same time, the rows and columns of the relevant planets are colored in the **aspect table just below**. **Click** the aspect symbol in a highlighted cell to see what that aspect means.
4. Pressing it again clears it. Pressing another theme switches to it (only one theme is ever lit).

!!! info "Plans, and when the birth time is unknown"
    - On the free plan you can try one theme, Career; the remaining five themes are available on Basic and above.
    - If the birth time is unknown, the houses cannot be determined, so the theme feature is not shown. Instead, click the cells of the aspect table and read their meanings.

## Glossary

- The button at the bottom left of the screen opens the **Glossary**.
- It has three tabs: **12 signs, 12 houses, and 5 aspects**. Look terms up as you read.
