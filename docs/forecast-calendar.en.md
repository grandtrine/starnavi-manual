# Forecast Calendar

!!! abstract "About this chapter"
    This chapter covers the Forecast Calendar. The Forecast Calendar is **your own one-year calendar** built from your birth chart (a set of two: a month-by-month calendar and a new moon reading calendar). Members can create it, view it, download the PDF, and add it to their device calendar within StarNavigator (**Basic plan and above**; the free plan, and calendars for anyone other than yourself, go through the external purchase page).

!!! note "The calendar itself is in Japanese"
    The Forecast Calendar screens, the generated PDFs, and the usage guide are provided in Japanese.

## Creating a calendar

![Creating a new calendar (start year, start month, house system, color theme, name on the cover)](assets/forecast-calendar-01-create.jpg)

### Steps

1. Open the creation screen from **Readings** in the header, then **Create / Buy** on the Forecast Calendar card.
2. Press **Create a new calendar**.
3. Set the following.
    - **Start year** (2026 / 2027 / 2028)
    - **Start month** (January / April / your birth month)
    - **House system** (Placidus / Koch / Solar Sign)
    - **Color theme** (Standard / Legacy purple)
    - **Name on the cover** (optional; the name from your birth data if left blank)
4. Press the generate button (**8 credits**) and generation begins (it takes a few minutes to finish).

### Notes

- A calendar can only be created for **yourself** — the birth data you nominated as "This is my own data". Nominate one record in [Clients](birth-data.md).
- **When the birth time is unknown, the calendar is built with the solar sign house system.**
- **The 2028 edition is January-start only.**
- If you do not have enough credits, **Purchase Credits** takes you to your ARI account page to buy more.
- Calendars for anyone other than yourself, and calendars for free-plan users, are bought from the external purchase page. There is a **PDF edition (JPY 4,200, tax included)** and a **printed edition (JPY 8,000, tax included)**, and both **arrive as a PDF download link by email**. The printed edition also comes with a desk calendar sent by post.
- **Being able to view it inside StarNavigator at any time**, and the **calendar integration** described below, are only available when you create your own calendar inside StarNavigator. They are not available with the externally purchased calendar.

## Viewing and downloading

![The viewing screen (the month-by-month calendar, the PDF download, and the switch to the new moon calendar)](assets/forecast-calendar-02-view.jpg)

### Steps

1. When generation finishes, the **PDF is displayed directly** on the screen (on a smartphone you can pinch to zoom).
2. The PDF download button (A5 landscape) saves the PDF.
3. The link to the new moon calendar switches to the new moon reading calendar.

### Notes

- Calendars you have created can be redisplayed at any time from the list of your calendars (their state is "viewable", "generating", or "failed").
- The same combination of start year and start month is generated only once, and redisplaying it consumes no credits.
- The **usage guide** on the screen explains how to read the month-by-month calendar and the new moon calendar.
    ![The usage guide (how to read the month-by-month and new moon calendars)](assets/forecast-calendar-03-guide.jpg)

## Adding it to your phone or computer calendar

### Steps

- **iPhone and iPad**: press the button for adding to the iPhone/iPad calendar and follow the on-screen instructions. A dedicated calendar is created and the events are added (by subscription).
- **Computer and Android**: save the file with the .ics download, then import it from Google Calendar under Settings, Import & export.

### Notes

- New moons, full moons, sign ingresses, and the dates that matter specifically to you are added to your calendar.
- Your device is detected automatically. The instructions for the other kind of device can be opened from the link provided.
- This calendar integration (the iPhone/iPad subscription and the .ics import) is **only available for a calendar created inside StarNavigator**. It is not available for one bought from the external purchase page.

!!! info "Plans and credits"
    Creating, viewing, the PDF, and the calendar integration inside StarNavigator require **Basic and above** (the free plan, and calendars for anyone other than yourself, go through the external purchase page). Each generation consumes **8 credits**.

!!! info "How it differs from the external purchase page"
    The external purchase page offers a **PDF edition (JPY 4,200, tax included)** and a **printed edition (JPY 8,000, tax included)**, and both **arrive as a PDF download link by email**. The printed edition also comes with a desk calendar sent by post. **Viewing it inside StarNavigator at any time, and the calendar integration with your phone or computer, are only available when the calendar is created inside StarNavigator**; they are not available with an external purchase.
